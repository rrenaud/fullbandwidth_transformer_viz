An explainer of Full-Bandwidth Transformer, arXiv:2608.08888

One grid, two orders

Every hidden state h in a decoder sits at a position t and a layer ℓ. Training fills this grid one row at a time. Inference fills it one column at a time, yet still obeys the reading rule that the row order made necessary — even though nothing forces it to. All three regimes are stacked below so you can watch them side by side.
